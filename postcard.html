Postcard

A physical postcard to hand out and leave in cafés, studios and shops. Below is a ready visual example of both sides at the correct proportions, with full specs underneath. Replicate it in Canva, or use the Canva design I prepared.

Format: A6 landscape, 148 × 105 mm (the standard postcard size — easy to print, easy to mail, fits any rack). Add 3 mm bleed on every side for printing (so set canvas to 154 × 111 mm) and keep all text 5 mm inside the edges. Print at 300 DPI, full colour, both sides.

Design specs for Canva

  • Canvas: 154 × 111 mm landscape (148 × 105 mm + 3 mm bleed all sides), 300 DPI
  • Colours: gold #F2B75C · cream #F0E6CF · blue #7FD0EA · dark forest #0A1411
  • Fonts: title in a clean sans (Outfit / Montserrat), body in the same sans light; keep it consistent with the website
  • Front: full-bleed dancer image, title centered, date + facilitators at the bottom over a dark gradient so text stays readable
  • Back: short text on the left, QR code on the right linking to ples-duse.org, "Scan to register"
  • QR: dark code on cream background, minimum 25 × 25 mm so phones scan it reliably
  • Keep all text at least 5 mm away from the trim edge
